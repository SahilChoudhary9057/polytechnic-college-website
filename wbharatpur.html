About Us

This college was established specially for women in 2010 in Bharatpur division of Rajasthan. The aim for establishment of this college was to impart valuable/employable education to the girls in sub urban areas. The college initially started with 3 branches in which two branches were of non engineering courses namely 'Textile Design' and 'CostumeDesign and Dress Making' and one engineering branch namely 'Computer Science & Engineering'. Later one more enginnering branch (Electrical Engineering) was opened in the college.
