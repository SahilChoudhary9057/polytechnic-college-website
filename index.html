Welcome to our website providing information about Government Polytechnic Colleges.


Candidates who are eligible for admission to the first year of diploma engineering courses in the entire state and private academic polytechnic colleges of Rajasthan are invited to apply online or optional form for admission through the centralized system. Download the guidelines and brochure from the departmental website http://date.rajasthan.gov.in, study it thoroughly and fill the online application form. The candidate must have appeared in the Madhyamik examination or equivalent examination of the Secondary Education Board with at least 35% marks.
