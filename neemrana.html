About Us

Government Polytechnic college Neemrana is the first Polytechnic college in rural area in the state Rajasthan.It is located in a small village called KundanSingh Pura which is 4 km far from Neemrana Tehsil.,Neemrana is a magnificent town with great heritage and natural scenic beauty located in the state of Rajasthan and is aptly placed on 2 Km far from the NH-8 (Delhi-Jaipur Highway) that is under the umbrella of Delhi- NCR. and college is situated at 6 KM far from NH8 Delhi-Jaipur Highway.
