About Us

Government Women Polytechnic College, Ajmer established in the year 1988 by the Board of Technical Education, Govt. of Rajasthan is one of the leading Institute with a strong academic and curricular support with effective and dedicated faculty to impart quality education. It has emerged as a leading institute in the educational hub providing women an opportunity to play a positive role in the society. The College is backed by the strong student strength successful in every walk of life.
